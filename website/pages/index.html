Characterizations of E-finite algebras and conjectures on brick-finiteness

Companion formalization to the manuscript by Haruhisa Enomoto (November 25, 2026)

A finite-dimensional algebra Λ over a field k is E-finite when its two-term silting (g-vector) fan Σ(Λ) ⊆ K0(proj Λ)ℝ contains every integral lattice point K0(proj Λ). This library formalizes in Mathlib-only Lean 4 that E-finiteness is equivalent over an arbitrary field to seventeen module-theoretic, categorical, numerical, and homological conditions—unifying seven open conjectures on brick-finiteness for each individual algebra—and proves this common conjecture in rank at most two over every field.

1. Background and the seven brick-finiteness conjectures

For a finite-dimensional k-algebra Λ of rank n = |sim Λ|, a fundamental theorem of Demonet, Iyama, and Jasso and of Asai identifies three global finiteness conditions: Λ has finitely many isomorphism classes of finite-dimensional bricks (brick-finiteness) if and only if it is τ-tilting finite, and if and only if its g-vector fan Σ(Λ) covers all of K0(proj Λ)ℝ.

Because silting cones are rational polyhedral cones, K0(proj Λ) ⊆ Σ(Λ) holds precisely when Σ(Λ) contains every rational ray (rational completeness, or vanishing of Derksen–Fei's generic self E-invariant when k = k̄). Demonet's lattice point conjecture asks whether rational completeness already forces the fan to be complete—that is, whether every E-finite algebra is brick-finite. In parallel, six other open problems ask whether brick-finiteness follows from:

  • finiteness of every semibrick in mod Λ (Enomoto's finite semibrick conjecture), or uniqueness of the n simple modules among n-element semibricks (Mousavand–Paquette);
  • having only finitely many bricks of each endolength r ≥ 1 (second brick-Brauer–Thrall conjecture), or of each endolength at each integral stability weight θ (Pfeifer's stable Brauer–Thrall II′ conjecture);
  • nonexistence of infinite-dimensional generic bricks in Mod Λ (Mousavand–Paquette's generic brick conjecture);
  • functorial finiteness of every wide subcategory of mod Λ (Angeleri Hügel–Sentieri).

Theorem A(I) proves that all seven hypotheses are equivalent to E-finiteness for each fixed algebra Λ over any field k, reducing all seven conjectures pointwise to a single problem.

2. Principal formalized theorems

Theorem A — Characterizations of E-finite algebras Characterizations/Equivalences.lean ↗

Let Λ be a finite-dimensional k-algebra of rank n.

I(a). Dimension vectors & torsion classes (any k)

  • (E-fin) K0(proj Λ) ⊆ Σ(Λ).
  • (K₀-Hom) [M] = [N] ≠ 0 ⇒ HomΛ(M, N) ≠ 0.
  • (mon-dim) M(𝒯) → K0(mod Λ) injective for every torsion class 𝒯.
  • (comp-tors) Every compact torsion class is functorially finite.
  • (num-disj) Every torsion pair is numerically disjoint.

I(b). Wide subcategories & brick counts (any k)

  • (wide), (wide₁) Every wide subcategory (or Filt(B)) is functorially finite.
  • (brick-label) Every brick labels a cover in f-tors Λ.
  • (fin-sbrick), (n-sbrick) Semibricks are finite; simples are the unique n-semibrick.
  • (brick-discrete), (endo-stab), (poly-brick) Endolength finiteness and polynomial length growth.

I(c). Large modules & localizations (any k)

  • (no-gen-brick), (no-gen-grain) No generic brick or generic grain in Mod Λ.
  • (thick-gen) Every thick subcategory of K[−1,0](proj Λ) has a presilting generator.
  • (univ-loc), (ring-epi) Universal localizations and simple Artinian epic targets are finite-dimensional.

II & III. Infinite and algebraically closed fields

  • k infinite: (dim-brick) and (dim-stab) (finitely many bricks / θ-stable bricks per dimension vector d ∈ ℕn).
  • k = k̄: (moduli-pt) (|ℳθss(Λ, d)| ≤ 1), (open-brick) (open brick orbits), and (open-wide) (open wide loci).
Theorem B — Collapse to brick-finiteness in rank at most two Silting/Fans/RankTwoTauTilting.lean ↗

Let Λ be a finite-dimensional algebra of rank n ≤ 2 over an arbitrary field k. Then Λ is E-finite if and only if Λ is brick-finite (equivalently, τ-tilting finite). In particular, all seven conjectures hold in rank at most two over every field.

Corollary C — Weyman's conjecture in arbitrary characteristic Geometry/ComponentMultiplicity.lean ↗

Over an algebraically closed field k of any characteristic, Λ is Schur-representation-finite (dim-brick) if and only if it is multiplicity-free (on every irreducible component of every representation variety rep(Λ, d), each GL(d)-semi-invariant weight space has dimension at most one).

Two further body corollaries are proved in full generality: Corollary 4.3 characterizes E-finiteness by cancellativity of the Grothendieck monoids of all torsion classes—or, dually, all torsion-free classes—in mod Λ (MonoidCancellation.lean); and Corollary 7.9 shows that if every ring epimorphism Λ → Γ with Tor1Λ(Γ, Γ) = 0 has an Artin algebra target, then Λ is E-finite, answering questions of Angeleri Hügel–Marks–Vitória and Angeleri Hügel–Sentieri in rank ≤ 2 (ArtinTargets.lean).
