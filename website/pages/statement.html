1. Complete reference for Theorem A

Let Λ be a finite-dimensional algebra over a field k of rank n = |sim Λ|. Baseline condition (E-fin) states that every integral class g = [P0] − [P−1] ∈ K0(proj Λ) is represented by a two-term presilting complex (HomKb(proj Λ)(U, U[1]) = 0).

2. Theorem B, Corollary C, and the five Challenge endpoints

Challenge.lean checks five standalone theorems against Solution.lean:

  1. Challenge.arbitraryField — Theorem A(I): the seventeen arbitrary-field equivalences above.
  2. Challenge.infiniteField — Theorem A(II): the two dimension-vector equivalences (dim-brick) and (dim-stab) over infinite fields.
  3. Challenge.algebraicallyClosedField — Theorem A(III): existence of a split basic Morita chart (Nonempty (Chart k R)) and the equivalences with (moduli-pt), (open-brick), and (open-wide) across all such charts.
  4. Challenge.rankTwo — Theorem B: when |sim Λ| ≤ 2 over any field, Λ is E-finite iff Λ is τ-tilting finite iff Λ is brick-finite.
  5. Challenge.brickDiscreteMultiplicityFree — Corollary C (Weyman's conjecture): over an algebraically closed field of any characteristic, (dim-brick) holds iff every GL(d)-semi-invariant weight space on every irreducible component of rep(Λ, d) has dimension ≤ 1.

3. Encoding conventions in Statement.lean

Why a separate Statement.lean? A large proof library inevitably introduces internal helper structures. Stating the endpoints directly over Mathlib in Statement.lean (~625 lines) lets a human mathematician verify that the formalized definitions match the paper without auditing 388 proof files.

  • Right modules via opposite rings: Right modules over a finite-dimensional k-algebra R are left modules over Rᵐᵒᵖ: Mod Rᵐᵒᵖ := FGModuleCat.{u} Rᵐᵒᵖ for mod Λ and ModuleCat.{u} Rᵐᵒᵖ for Mod Λ.
  • Projective lattice & integral weights: Lattice A is the quotient of the free abelian group on finitely generated projectives by isomorphism and biproduct relations, and integral weights are pairs P Q : Proj A acting on M via dim_k Hom(Q, M) - dim_k Hom(P, M).
  • Dimension vectors & Grothendieck monoids: K₀ A is the quotient of the free abelian group on Mod A by short exact sequences. For a torsion class T, ExactMonoidRel T generates the additive congruence of short exact sequences inside T on Multiset {X // T X}.
  • Two-term thick subcategories: Differential.realize places a map P ⟶ Q in degrees −1 and 0 of HomotopyCategory (Mod A) (ComplexShape.up ℤ); equivalence between the triangulated envelope in K(mod A) and in Kᵇ(proj A) is proved in BoundedProjectiveHomotopy.lean.
  • Basic Morita charts and King's Proj: Over k = k̄, representation varieties require vertex idempotents of a basic algebra. Chart k R packages any split basic k-algebra C.A equipped with a complete set of primitive orthogonal idempotents and an equivalence Mod Rᵐᵒᵖ ≌ Mod C.A, constructs kingProj = AlgebraicGeometry.Proj (kingGrading …) directly from Mathlib's projective spectrum, and proves both existence of a chart and invariance across all charts.
