# Provenance

Haruhisa Enomoto authored the manuscript and maintains the formalization. The Lean development was produced with OpenAI Codex under his direction. Its coverage follows the [November 25 manuscript](docs/MANUSCRIPT.md). The manuscript credits several AI-assisted research threads, including the arbitrary-field equivalences, the generic-module and localization arguments, the rank-two proof and the equal-class extraction. The [proof guide](docs/proofs/PROOF-ROUTES.md) describes the arguments implemented in Lean.

The Lean kernel checks the proof terms with Mathlib at revision `c55e6e786f49471c72fbddbec5415808896aec1e`. No independent human review of the entire Lean development is recorded. Adapted proofs are credited by module in the [source inventory](verification/module-origins.json). The Lean sources are available under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
