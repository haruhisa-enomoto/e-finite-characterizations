# Formalized manuscript

This repository formalizes the manuscript

> Haruhisa Enomoto, *Characterizations of E-finite algebras and conjectures on
> brick-finiteness*, frozen version of November 25, 2026 (43 pages).

The formalized revision contains Theorem A (parts I, II, and III), Theorem B
(`= Theorem 8.4`), Corollary C (Weyman's conjecture), and 41 body and appendix
theorems, propositions, lemmas, and corollaries (44 numbered statements in total).
Every numbered statement is linked to its Lean declarations in
[`PAPER-CORRESPONDENCE.md`](PAPER-CORRESPONDENCE.md).
