Theorems and definitions

Statements

Let Λ be a finite-dimensional algebra over a field k. Its projective lattice is the split Grothendieck group of finitely generated projective right modules. E-finiteness means that the support of the two-term silting fan contains each point of that lattice.

Theorem A

Over an arbitrary field the paper gives seventeen conditions equivalent to E-finiteness. They concern the equal-class Hom criterion, torsion monoids and torsion pairs, wide subcategories, brick labels and semibricks, endolength and polynomial growth, generic modules, thick generation, universal localizations and simple Artinian epic targets. Two further fixed-vector tests hold over infinite fields. Three tests over algebraically closed fields use King moduli, brick orbits and wide loci.

Theorem B and Corollary C

In rank at most two, E-finiteness, brick-finiteness and τ-tilting finiteness are equivalent. Over an algebraically closed field, finiteness of the bricks of each composition vector is equivalent to multiplicity-freeness on the irreducible components of the representation varieties.
