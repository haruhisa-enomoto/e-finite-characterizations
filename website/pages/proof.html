Proof guide

From the silting fan to bricks

Inside a cone

At an interior weight of a presilting cone, the semistable category identifies with a perpendicular module category. The classes of its simple objects are linearly independent. This prevents two distinct stable objects at that weight from having the same composition vector.

A missing integral class

Represent the class by a differential between finite projectives. Failure of presilting gives a nonzero map from the cokernel to the Nakayama kernel. A map of minimum image dimension produces a stable brick. Varying the differential gives witnesses violating the finiteness criteria. Over finite fields the proof descends stable constituents along Frobenius orbits and counts their ground forms.

Rank two

In each mixed quadrant, insert the sum of two boundary vectors between them. A shifted Hom dimension bounds the depth of the resulting subdivision. The finite cover gives a bound on the presilting cokernels and finitely many weights that detect every brick.

Artin targets

A missing weight yields a universal localization with an infinite-dimensional quotient detected by finite-dimensional representations. The localization has vanishing first Tor. In an Artinian target the descending chain condition would make the quotient embed in a finite-dimensional representation. This proves Corollary 7.9.
