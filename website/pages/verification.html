1. What is mechanically verified vs. what requires human reading

Following the Palomar verification architecture, statement review is separated from proof checking:

  • Standalone statement (Statement.lean → Challenge.lean): Imports Mathlib alone and states the five endpoints (Theorem A(I–III), Theorem B, and Corollary C) with five deliberate sorry placeholders.
  • Proof replay (Solution.lean + lake comparator): Proves all five declarations from the library without importing Challenge.lean. Running lake comparator inside Bubblewrap checks that every definition and theorem type in the transitive closure matches byte-for-byte, confirms zero extra axioms, and replays all proofs through the Lean 4 kernel as well as the external checkers NanoDa and con-ron.
  • Human review surface: No human expert has reviewed the 388 proof modules line-by-line. Because the kernel and external checkers guarantee soundness of the proofs, the sole mathematical judgment left to a human reader is verifying that the ~625 lines of Mathlib-only definitions in Statement.lean faithfully express the statements of the paper.

3. Reproducing the checks locally

python3 scripts/check-lean-sources.py
lake exe cache get
lake build
python3 scripts/audit_axioms.py
python3 scripts/scan_axioms.py
python3 scripts/generate_challenge.py --check
lake build Challenge Solution
./scripts/verify-comparator.sh

verify-comparator.sh runs on Linux with bwrap (Bubblewrap) on PATH and a real directory (not a symlink) at .lake/packages; see docs/VERIFICATION.md for details.
